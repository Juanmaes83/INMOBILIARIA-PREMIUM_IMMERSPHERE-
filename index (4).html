CH 01 — 00:04Manifesto
A house conceived from fire and water — and suspended, quite literally, between the two.

Villa Siena was not assembled. It was carved — over years, from teak and stone, on a hillside above the Bel Air Country Club.

At its heart, a four-story staircase finished in bronze hangs from the ceiling above a reflecting pool, appearing to float. Thirty-foot walls of glass glide open without sound, dissolving the boundary between architecture and canyon air. Every material was hand-selected; every line answers to nature rather than fashion.

This is not a residence designed to impress on arrival. It is designed to keep revealing itself — at dusk, in winter light, years after the first visit.
